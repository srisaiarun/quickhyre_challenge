Write the SAFETY FINDINGS section of the pharmacovigilance report.

Use only the supplied evidence.

Describe the most frequently reported reactions and their
observed case counts and percentages.

Also describe the reported outcome distribution.

Pay particular attention to the leading reactions and serious
reaction findings.

Do not claim that bisoprolol caused any reaction.

Do not call any reaction a confirmed safety signal.

Do not infer expectedness.

Return JSON with exactly this structure:

{
  "section": "safety_findings",
  "claims": [
    {
      "text": "...",
      "evidence_ids": ["EV-..."]
    }
  ]
}

Every factual claim MUST have at least one evidence ID.
