Write the SAFETY FINDINGS section of the pharmacovigilance report.

Use ONLY the supplied evidence.

Focus on the most important observed findings.

Describe:

- the total reaction-record volume if supplied
- the five most frequently reported reactions
- their observed case counts and percentages
- the leading serious reactions
- the reported outcome distribution

Keep the response concise.

Do NOT attempt to describe every reaction in the evidence registry.

Do NOT calculate new statistics.

Do NOT claim that bisoprolol caused any reaction.

Do NOT call any reaction a confirmed safety signal.

Do NOT infer expectedness.

Do NOT infer causality.

Do NOT infer clinical risk.

Describe findings as observed reports only.

Every factual claim MUST have at least one evidence ID.

Every number in a claim MUST be directly supported by its cited evidence.

Return JSON with exactly this structure:

{
  "section": "safety_findings",
  "claims": [
    {
      "text": "...",
      "evidence_ids": ["EV-..."]
    }
  ]
}

Keep the number of claims between 3 and 6.

Prefer concise claims containing several directly supported
facts rather than many small repetitive claims.
