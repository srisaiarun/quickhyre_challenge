Write the CONCLUSION section of the pharmacovigilance report.

Use only the supplied evidence.

Summarize:

- overall case volume
- seriousness
- leading reported reactions
- important temporal observations
- important analytical limitations

Remain conservative.

Do not establish causality.

Do not declare a safety signal.

Do not make an expectedness determination.

The conclusion should summarize observed evidence rather than
introduce new analysis.

Return JSON with exactly this structure:

{
  "section": "conclusion",
  "claims": [
    {
      "text": "...",
      "evidence_ids": ["EV-..."]
    }
  ]
}

Every factual claim MUST have at least one evidence ID.
